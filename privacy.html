Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

At ChemSpatial, we take your privacy extremely seriously. We believe that your data is exactly that—yours. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the ChemSpatial iOS application.

1. Local Processing

ChemSpatial operates entirely on-device. When you import, paste, or visualize molecular coordinate files (such as SDF or XYZ formats), all parsing and 3D rendering occurs locally on your iPhone or iPad hardware.

  • We do not upload your chemical models to any external servers.
  • We do not store your imported files in the cloud.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect, track, or sell any personal data or usage metrics.

Because the app does not require a user account to function, we do not have databases containing your name, email address, or phone number.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ChemSpatial offers an optional "Pro" subscription via Apple's App Store.

  • All transactions are securely handled by Apple's native StoreKit.
  • We do not have access to your credit card information or billing details.
  • Apple provides us with an anonymous receipt validation token to unlock premium features, which cannot be traced back to your personal identity.

4. Third-Party Services

ChemSpatial does not embed any third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs (such as AdMob or Facebook Pixel), or external monetization tools (such as RevenueCat). The app relies purely on native iOS frameworks.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or App Store guidelines. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
